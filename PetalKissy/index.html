our story

Romance, reworn.

Petal Kiss Closet began with a simple heartbreak: watching beautiful garments — worn once, loved briefly — end up forgotten. We believed every piece deserves a second love story.

Today, we hunt down the finest preloved fashion across the Philippines, put each find through a meticulous 12-point inspection, gentle steam, and fresh photography — then match it with someone who’ll adore it next. It’s luxury made affordable, and fashion made kind.
